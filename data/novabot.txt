Nova Robotics — NovaBot X1 Product Overview

The NovaBot X1 is a household assistant robot released by Nova Robotics in March 2024.
It is designed for indoor use only and is not waterproof. The recommended operating
temperature range is 10 to 35 degrees Celsius.

Battery and charging: The NovaBot X1 uses a 5200 mAh lithium-ion battery. On a full
charge it runs for approximately 6 hours of normal use. A full charge takes 90 minutes
using the included 24V charger. The battery is not user-replaceable and must be serviced
by an authorized Nova Robotics center.

Warranty: The NovaBot X1 comes with an 18 month limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover water damage, physical impact damage, or battery
degradation after 12 months. Extended warranty of up to 36 months can be purchased
within 30 days of the original purchase date.

Support: Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM India Standard Time.
The support email is support@novarobotics.example. Phone support is available only for
customers who purchased the NovaBot X1 directly from the Nova Robotics website.

Firmware updates are released every quarter and are installed automatically over Wi-Fi.
Users can delay an update by up to 7 days from the notification.
